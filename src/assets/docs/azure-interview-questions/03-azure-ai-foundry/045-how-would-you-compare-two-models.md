# How would you compare two models?

## Short answer
Compare two models on identical inputs with quantitative and human evidence.

## Key points
- Same dataset and comparable prompts, then prompt-tuned runs.
- Metrics table for quality, latency, cost and safety.
- Pairwise LLM-as-judge with position swapping plus human review of a sample.
- Confirm with a canary or A/B test.

## CWD context
Record the decision and the evidence in the model inventory.
