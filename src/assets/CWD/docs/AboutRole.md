My experience as a Solution Architect has mainly been around taking business requirements and transforming them into scalable AI solutions.

In my current role, I work closely with business stakeholders and engineering teams to understand the problem, identify where AI can add value, and define the overall solution approach. I’m involved throughout the complete lifecycle—from requirement gathering and use-case definition to architecture, development, testing, deployment, and production support.

A major part of my recent experience has been designing enterprise Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions. For complex business processes, I’ve worked on architectures where multiple specialized agents collaborate to complete a task. This includes defining the responsibilities of each component, how they communicate, how information is retrieved, how tools are accessed, and how the overall workflow is controlled.

At the same time, I remain hands-on with the implementation. I work closely with developers and also contribute to development myself, including building and reviewing components, integrating AI models and services, implementing RAG and agent workflows, developing APIs, troubleshooting issues, and improving the overall solution. I’m also involved in testing, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and resolving production issues.

I also take ownership of the non-functional aspects of the solution, including scalability, security, reliability, performance, observability, cost, and governance. I work with the engineering team to make sure the architecture is practical to implement and can be successfully taken into production.

So, I would describe my role as a combination of **solution architecture, hands-on engineering, and technical leadership**. I can work at the architecture level with stakeholders, but I’m also comfortable going deep into the implementation and working alongside the engineering team to deliver the solution end to end.
